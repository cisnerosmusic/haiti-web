Press

Since 2006 the Norwegian press has followed his work, from national dailies such as Klassekampen and Bergens Tidende to the local papers of Bodø, Sogn and Stavanger. The articles are kept in the digital archive of the National Library of Norway.

Nordmenn er opptekne av lyset, kubanarar er meir opptekne av fargane.

Norwegians care about the light; Cubans care more about the colours.

Ramón Eduardo Haití Filiu to Firda, 10 September 2012
