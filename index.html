Born in Havana in 1971, trained at San Alejandro and based in Bergen since 2009, Ramón Eduardo Haití Filiu builds his paintings from paint, printed fabric, jute and corrugated cardboard: figures in wide-brimmed hats, fish, masks, the sea.
